Build the three additive frontend pieces the app shell will wire together in 06-04: the operation-capability descriptor (`lib/operations.ts`), the six-variant `CategorySwitcher`, and the generalized virtualized `CategoryList` (the `NotesList` machinery generalized over `BrowseRow` + a category). Purpose: one virtualized list, one perf story, one selection model, one capability map — reusing the established dark-token/44px design language, inventing no second design system.
Output: `operations.ts` (+ test), `CategorySwitcher.tsx` (+ test), `CategoryList.tsx` (+ test migrating the NotesList assertions).
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
