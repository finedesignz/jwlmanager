<objective>
Prove the two things a fold can do that a single Phase 5 merge structurally cannot:
fail halfway through, and lose an intermediate step's media. Then spend the harness
this phase needed anyway on closing — or honestly re-documenting — Phase 5's
recorded playlist-merge coverage gap.

Purpose: an N-way operation has N-1 chances to fail partway. The user must never
be shown, or have promoted, a "merged 2 of 3" archive. This is the Core Value's
sharpest edge in the phase and it gets its own plan rather than riding along.

Output: the failure-isolation, media, and playlist test bodies, plus a recorded
finding for D10-06 either way.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-10-07 | Tampering | a corrupt/hostile source aborting the fold mid-chain | high | mitigate | `fold_step_failure_pristine` asserts the live DB and all N sources are byte-identical afterwards and the fold root is removed; no promote occurs on any failure path |
| T-10-08 | Denial of service | staging accumulation across repeated failed folds | medium | mitigate | Single `remove_dir_all` on the fold ROOT on every exit path, asserted after two consecutive forced failures |
